Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 October 2026 (previous version: 17 September 2026). App: Kinbowl, com.kinbowl.app.

In short

This app collects nothing about you. It has no servers, no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Everything you enter stays in your phone's storage.

There is one narrow exception: when you search for a treat by brand or scan a barcode, the app asks the open food database Open Food Facts. Only the search text or the barcode number is sent. No profile, weight, cycle data or device identifier. The rest of the app works offline.

To calculate your daily targets and build a meal plan, the app asks for:

All of it is stored on your device only, in the app's standard storage. We cannot see this data and have no access to it.

Health data

Date of birth, weight, height — and cycle or menopause settings above all — are sensitive. The app's rule is absolute: cycle data never leaves the device. It is not synced, not backed up to our servers (there are none) and not shared with anyone.

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Its calculations follow widely accepted formulas and guidelines; if you have a medical condition, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a doctor.

Notifications

Shopping-day and cooking-day reminders are scheduled locally, by your own phone. We send no push notifications and therefore know nothing about your device. Permission is requested only when you switch reminders on, and you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings.

Treat search and barcode scanner

To find a treat of a specific brand and flavor, the app queries Open Food Facts, an open, non-profit food database (openfoodfacts.org). This happens only when you type a search or scan a barcode yourself.

What is sentWhat is NOT sent
The text you typed ("milka") or the barcode numberName, profile, weight, height, age, goal, cycle, plan, fridge
The app's country, so local brands come firstAny device or advertising identifier

Like any internet request, it reaches the server together with your connection's IP address. How Open Food Facts handles such requests is described in their privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

The treat you pick (name, brand, calories) is stored on your phone, like the rest of your data.

Camera. The scanner uses the camera only to read the barcode. It takes no photos and stores nothing. Permission is requested at the first scan and can be withdrawn in your phone's settings. Without it everything else still works: you can search for the treat or enter it by hand.

Children and teenagers

You can create a profile for a minor — usually filled in by an adult in the family. That data likewise stays on the device. For minors the app does not allow a weight-loss goal or manual macro editing, under any subscription.

Deleting your data

  • "Start over" in Settings erases profiles, the current plan and fridge contents. Country, kitchen equipment, reminders and subscription state remain.
  • Uninstalling the app erases everything without a trace. Because no copies exist on any server, the data is then gone for good — recoverable by no one, including us.

There is no separate deletion request to file, and no one to file it with: your data never left your phone.

Third parties

One: Open Food Facts, only for treat search and barcodes, as described above. The app contains no ad networks, no analytics, no social SDKs and no tracking technology.

When paid subscriptions arrive, payment will be handled by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your card details. This document will be updated before subscriptions go live.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts sending anything off your device, this page will be changed in advance — not after the fact — and the app will say so.
