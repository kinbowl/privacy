Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 September 2026. App: Kinbowl, com.kinbowl.app.

In short

This app collects nothing about you. It has no servers, no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Everything you enter stays in your phone's storage and is never sent anywhere.

This is not a promise on paper: the app's code contains no network requests at all. It works entirely offline.

What you enter, and where it lives

To calculate your daily targets and build a meal plan, the app asks for:

WhatWhy
A name (any word or nickname will do)To tell family profiles apart
Sex, date of birth, height, weightTo calculate daily calorie and protein targets
Activity level, training frequency, goalSame
Your own calorie or protein figure, if you set oneSame
Diet, excluded foods, preferencesSo the app never suggests what you don't eat
Menstrual cycle settings, if you turn them onLuteal-phase calorie adjustment and micronutrient hints
What's already in your fridgeTo subtract it from the shopping list
Kitchen equipment, country and storeTo skip dishes you can't cook, and show prices in your currency
Your daily treatTo shrink the rest of the day's budget around it

All of it is stored on your device only, in the app's standard storage. We cannot see this data and have no access to it.

Health data

Date of birth, weight, height — and menstrual cycle settings above all — are sensitive. The app's rule is absolute: cycle data never leaves the device. It is not synced, not backed up to our servers (there are none) and not shared with anyone.

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Its calculations follow widely accepted formulas and guidelines; if you have a medical condition, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a doctor.

Notifications

Shopping-day and cooking-day reminders are scheduled locally, by your own phone. We send no push notifications and therefore know nothing about your device. Permission is requested only when you switch reminders on, and you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings.

Children and teenagers

You can create a profile for a minor — usually filled in by an adult in the family. That data likewise stays on the device. For minors the app does not allow a weight-loss goal or manual macro editing, under any subscription.

Deleting your data

  • "Start over" in Settings erases profiles, the current plan and fridge contents. Country, kitchen equipment, reminders and subscription state remain.
  • Uninstalling the app erases everything without a trace. Because no copies exist on any server, the data is then gone for good — recoverable by no one, including us.

There is no separate deletion request to file, and no one to file it with: your data never left your phone.

Third parties

As of today, none. The app contains no ad networks, no analytics, no social SDKs and no tracking technology.

When paid subscriptions arrive, payment will be handled by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your card details. This document will be updated before subscriptions go live.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts sending anything off your device, this page will be changed in advance — not after the fact — and the app will say so.
